# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

LlamaCon99 is a community project for people building and using the app on Android and iOS. We are
committed to making participation welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, or platform.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to feedback about impact.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish anyone's personal information, contact data, or private
  communications without permission.
- Focus feedback on what helps users and improves the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized
attention without consent, publishing private information, and sustained disruptive behavior are
not acceptable. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project
spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct. They may
remove or edit content, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. They will
consider the impact and context of each incident and aim to communicate enforcement decisions
clearly.

To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately through the [project owner's GitHub
profile](https://github.com/albertolicea00). Please include relevant links or context and avoid
sharing sensitive information publicly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
